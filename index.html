My Laado,

100 days ago, we became something more than two people who met in a Roblox game. But our story had already been growing for months before that — through daily talks, silly games, little surprises, and all those moments when time just disappeared.

I still remember the flower proposal, the message that got tagged, and the long day on Instagram before you finally said yes. I remember making things for you, building our pink garden, watching our love tree grow, and seeing how much effort you put into our matching Zepeto outfits.

Thank you for supporting me, laughing with me, playing with me, and making ordinary days feel special. Thank you for being my Laado, my Bchi, my Maalkin — and for calling me your Pookie, Chikna and Gulaam. Every nickname has a little memory attached to it.

We may be far apart, but all these little worlds we have made together remind me how much happiness we can create, even from two different screens. I don't want this story to end at 100 days. I want to keep adding chapters, one memory at a time.

Happy 100 days, Laado. Apke saath meri favourite story abhi shuru hui hai.

Always your Pookie ♡
